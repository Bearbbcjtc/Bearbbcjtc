# Hao Wang

**AI agents · Visual analytics · Scientific software**

I am a PhD candidate in **Computer Graphics Technology** at **Purdue University**, in the **School of Applied and Creative Computing**, expecting to graduate in **August 2027**. I build software that connects complex research data, analytical methods, and interactive interfaces.

My work spans protocol-grounded AI agents, biomedical research infrastructure, and graph learning for geospatial visualization. I care about making analytical decisions inspectable and evaluating software with reproducible tests and controlled experiments.

## Selected research and engineering

### llmAidVis — Protocol-grounded clinical analytics

A Python research system that connects a persistent clinical knowledge graph to protocol alignment, data binding, statistical analysis, and visualization.

- Implemented tool dispatch, typed analysis contracts, cohort and endpoint checks, and execution receipts.
- Integrated Gen3 GraphQL retrieval with a Streamlit interface.
- Evaluated statistical method selection, subject-level event construction, and deferral of unsupported analyses.

**Focus:** AI agents, knowledge graphs, analytical validation, and interactive visualization.

### GeoProject — Healthcare accessibility maps

Research on graph-based coordinate learning and map deformation using travel-time objectives, geographic constraints, and topology checks.

- Built experiments comparing equivariant networks, graph transformers, and direct optimization.
- Developed controlled comparisons and ablations to distinguish deformation response, map fidelity, and topology preservation.

**Focus:** PyTorch, graph learning, geospatial analysis, and experimental evaluation.

### ARDaC / Anagine — Biomedical research software

Contributed to clinical research workflows and the ARDaC data commons, including frontend and API integration, visualization, testing, deployment, monitoring, and maintenance.

- Developed and integrated Anagine AI features into the ARDaC portal as part of a research team.
- Connected research interfaces to Gen3 data access and GraphQL queries.
- Worked with Docker, Kubernetes, GitOps, and AWS EKS to support software delivery.

**Public code:** [Gen3 portal frontend snapshot](https://github.com/Bearbbcjtc/anagine-data-portal) · [AlcHepNet website development](https://github.com/Bearbbcjtc/AlcHepNet-website-dev)

These public repositories are development snapshots; they are not a complete release of the current research systems.
